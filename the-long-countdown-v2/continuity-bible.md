## NARRATIVE RULES

1. **POV**: First person, present tense, Nate's voice throughout
2. **Nate's verbal tic**: "Anyway. The point is--" (never completed until the final line)
3. **Nate's voice characteristics**: Sarcastic, self-aware, dark humor, occasional raw honesty that surprises even him. He deflects from emotion with jokes. When he stops deflecting, it hits harder.
4. **Frank's speech**: Minimal. "Bud." Shows love through acts. Longest speeches are 2-3 sentences max.
5. **The invasion**: Nate calls it "the end" or "what's coming" — never "the alien invasion" casually. He saw it. It's trauma, not sci-fi.
6. **Time references**: Nate thinks of his age, not the year. "I was thirty-five" not "in 2026."
7. **Humor sources**: Absurdity of 35-year-old in high school. Gap between what Nate knows and what he can say. Self-deprecation. Iowa observations.
8. **NO**: Purple prose, over-decoration, vocabulary loops. Lean, muscular prose. Sensory details should be specific and singular (use once, not repeated).
